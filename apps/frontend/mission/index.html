t. A PUBLIC FORUM

WHY THINKINK EXISTS

Where attention gathers,
discussion begins.

Major events and emerging trends capture public attention. Thinkink channels that attention into a shared public forum, bringing people together around the same topic to exchange views, ask questions, and take part in an informed discussion.

01 / RELEVANCE

Focus on what
draws attention.

We focus on events and trends with significant media attention. These moments of shared interest create an opportunity to bring people together and give public discussion a common starting point.

✳
02 / CONNECTION

Bring people
into one forum.

Thinkink’s purpose is to bring people following the same event into a shared space for discussion. News sources and topic overviews provide context, helping participants follow developments and contribute to a common conversation.

↗
03 / PARTICIPATION

Turn shared interest
into participation.

We are building a public forum where people can contribute their views, ask questions, and engage with different perspectives. Our ambition is to turn the visibility of a major event into active participation and constructive exchange.
